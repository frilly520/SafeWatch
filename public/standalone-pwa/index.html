Install safeWatch on iPhone

Add safeWatch to your Home Screen for a full-screen standalone camera experience without Safari toolbars:

  1. 1 Tap the Safari Share icon in the bottom bar.
  2. 2 Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
